About Us

Welcome to the AI and Next-generation Wireless Communications Laboratory at YTU. Our research focuses on cutting-edge technologies in wireless communications, network optimization and integrating AI with next-generation communication systems. We strive to push the boundaries of what's possible in the field of telecommunications.

Our interdisciplinary team combines expertise in signal processing, machine learning, and network architecture to solve complex challenges in modern communication systems.

Director's Message

Dr

A. F. M. Shahen Shah, PhD

Associate Professor, Department of ECE, Yildiz Technical university

Hello!
It is my pleasure to introduce the AI and Next-generation Wireless Communication Laboratory (ANWCL)—a hub of innovation at the intersection of artificial intelligence and advanced wireless technologies. Our slogan is Innovate. Integrate. Communicate. Currently, 5 projects are running, including TUBITAK 1001, TUBITAK 3501, TUBITAK 1005, and YTUBAP projects. Under these projects, 4 Ph.D., 6 Master and 4 Bachelor students are working in the lab. At ANWCL, we are committed to pushing the boundaries of wireless communication by integrating cutting-edge AI techniques. Our research focuses on developing intelligent, efficient, and future-ready communication systems that will shape the next era of connectivity. Our mission is to drive innovation, foster collaboration, and contribute to groundbreaking advancements in wireless technology. We welcome researchers, students, and industry partners to join us in shaping a smarter, more connected world.
Thank you, and welcome to ANWCL!
