About Us

Welcome to the Advanced Networking and Wireless Communications Laboratory at YTU. Our research focuses on cutting-edge technologies in wireless communications, network optimization, and next-generation communication systems. We strive to push the boundaries of what's possible in the field of telecommunications.

Our interdisciplinary team combines expertise in signal processing, machine learning, and network architecture to solve complex challenges in modern communication systems.

Director's Message

Dr

A. F. M. Shahen Shah, PhD

Laboratory Director & Professor

Welcome to ANWCL-YTU. It is my great pleasure to lead this exceptional team of researchers dedicated to advancing the frontiers of wireless communications and networking technologies. Our laboratory represents a convergence of innovative minds working collaboratively to address the most pressing challenges in modern telecommunications.

We are committed to fostering an environment of academic excellence, creative exploration, and impactful research that bridges theory and practical application. I invite you to explore our work and join us in shaping the future of communications technology.

Projects

Ongoing Projects

5G Network Optimization

Developing novel algorithms for optimizing 5G network performance in dense urban environments using machine learning techniques.

IoT Security Framework

Creating a comprehensive security framework for Internet of Things devices with focus on lightweight encryption and authentication.

Edge Computing Solutions

Investigating edge computing architectures to reduce latency and improve performance in real-time applications.

AI-Driven Network Management

Implementing artificial intelligence techniques for autonomous network management and optimization.

Finished Projects

Quantum Communications

Explored quantum key distribution protocols for ultra-secure communication channels in next-generation networks.

Smart Grid Communication

Developed reliable communication protocols for smart grid infrastructure enabling efficient energy distribution.

Vehicular Network Protocol

Designed and tested V2V communication protocols for enhanced road safety and traffic management.

Wireless Sensor Networks

Implemented energy-efficient protocols for large-scale wireless sensor network deployments.
